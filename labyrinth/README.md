# Labyrinth Level (`labyrinth-staging`)

`labyrinth_notebook.ipynb` turns a Backrooms level design into a maze through a ZZ-correlated quantum graph state, and walks through shots, steps and coupling strength.

- `level_2_final_miami.json`: a 6 × 6 Backrooms Labyrinth level, with the saved result of a run on IBM Miami. The notebook runs its 4 × 5 corner on the emulator.

Family: Labyrinth (core engine in `graph/`).
