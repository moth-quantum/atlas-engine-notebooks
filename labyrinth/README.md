# Labyrinth

| Notebook | Engine | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `labyrinth_level_notebook.ipynb` | Labyrinth Level (`labyrinth-staging`) | Turns a level design into a maze through a ZZ-correlated quantum graph state, walks through difficulty, shots, steps and individual shots, then draws a saved 6 × 6 run from IBM hardware. |

- `easy.json`, `medium.json`, `hard.json`, `nightmare.json`: the 4 × 5 levels from Atlas's Labyrinth demo.
- `level_2_final_miami.json`: a 6 × 6 Backrooms Labyrinth level with the saved result of a run on IBM Miami (drawn, not re-run).
