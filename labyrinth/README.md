# Labyrinth Level (`labyrinth-staging`)

`labyrinth_notebook.ipynb` turns a level design into a maze through a ZZ-correlated quantum graph state, and walks through difficulty, shots, steps and individual shots, then draws a saved 6 × 6 run from IBM hardware.

- `easy.json`, `medium.json`, `hard.json`, `nightmare.json`: the 4 × 5 levels from Atlas's Labyrinth demo.
- `level_2_final_miami.json`: a 6 × 6 Backrooms Labyrinth level with the saved result of a run on IBM Miami (drawn, not re-run).

Family: Labyrinth.
